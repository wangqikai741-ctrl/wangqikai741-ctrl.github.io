I am a final-year undergraduate at the Belarusian State University of Informatics and Radioelectronics (BSUIR) in Minsk, in a 2+2 joint programme with Luoyang Normal University (LYNU), China. My research is in semantic communication: sending what a downstream task needs from an image, rather than every pixel. I am currently building rate-adaptive deep joint source–channel coding for UAV-based vehicle detection, advised by Jun Ma at BSUIR.

Interests: semantic and task-oriented communication, deep joint source–channel coding (DJSCC), and communication-efficient vision for UAVs.

Research

Rate-adaptive semantic communication for UAV vehicle detection

Undergraduate research, March 2026 – present

A detector that is accurate enough is usually too heavy to run on a small UAV, so frames have to be sent to a ground station over a noisy link. I designed a lightweight slimmable codec that serves five bandwidth ratios with a single set of weights (258K parameters), and a blind rate-adaptation loop that needs no pilot symbols. The detector at the ground station stays frozen, and the system is judged by detection accuracy (mAP) rather than image quality.

Early versions of the system, with a CNN encoder and a supernet covering three compression ratios, appeared in two sole-author papers in 2026 (see Publications).

Other projects

  • Gait assessment for Parkinson's disease from wearable insole signals

    Collaborative project with Hao Li and Jun Ma

    I implemented the feature-extraction and classification pipeline and a unified evaluation framework covering eight classical classifiers and six deep-learning baselines, and ran the validation: subject-grouped cross-validation, leave-one-subject-out testing and ablations, with Mann–Whitney U tests and FDR correction. Published in Biosensors (2026).

  • Comparing YOLOv9–YOLOv12 for aerial vehicle detection

    Team project, September – November 2025

    A four-person comparison of recent YOLO models on an 11-class private aerial vehicle dataset. I ran the YOLOv12 experiments, aggregated and analysed the team's results, wrote the paper and presented it in English at New Horizons 2025. This project raised the question behind my current research: the detector cannot easily run on the UAV itself.
